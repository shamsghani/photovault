PhotoVault

A private, local photo organizer — your photos never leave your computer.

What it does

Open any folder of photos and tag them by people, places, years or your own custom categories. Filter, search, find duplicates, run slideshows, and export filtered views as portable libraries or real folders of copies.

Features

  • Fast previews — small thumbnails are generated once and cached, so even huge folders stay smooth. Full resolution only loads when you open a photo.
  • Tag filters — combine tags (ALL / ANY) and exclude tags by clicking them a second time.
  • Folder & photo views — browse a flat grid or drill in folder by folder.
  • Continuity — tags persist locally; multiple PhotoVault JSONs in a folder are combined automatically; auto-save writes tags back next to your photos.
  • EXIF aware — capture date and GPS position are read from photos and shown in the viewer.

Shortcuts

←/→ navigate  ·  Enter add tag  ·  Space pause slideshow  ·  Esc close / clear  ·  Shift+click select

Privacy & storage

Everything is processed in your browser. Thumbnails, tags and preferences are stored locally (IndexedDB & browser storage). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
